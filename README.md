# 🛡️ VSPBM - Venables Babymetal Stake Pool & DRep

Welcome to the official repository for **VSPBM**. I am a dedicated Cardano Stake Pool Operator focused on infrastructure resilience and transparent governance. I was a DRep on pre-prod and will be migrating to mainnet when funds allow.

## 🚀 Mission Statement
My mission is to safeguard the Cardano network as much as I can by providing high-availability block production on independent hardware and ensuring that governance decisions are made with a "Technical First" mindset. 

We believe that Cardano’s strength lies in its decentralization; therefore, I operate entirely on **Bare Metal infrastructure**, avoiding the risks associated with centralized cloud providers.

## 🏛️ Governance Philosophy (DRep)
As a DRep , VSPBM follows these core principles:
- **Treasury Responsibility:** I advocate for funding core infrastructure and developer tooling over short-term marketing initiatives.
- **Small-SPO Alignment:** I support parameters that maintain a level playing field for single-pool operators, especially those, like me, who are extremely small. That's why I'm a member of xSPO, repo link and instructions [here](https://github.com/xSPO-Alliance/adapools-xspo-alliance)
